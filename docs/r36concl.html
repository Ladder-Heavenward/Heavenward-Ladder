Colour is a blindness coloured in

A spectrum is a function, and its colour is three of its coefficients. Mixing adds them, the purples close the band into a circle, brightness and saturation fill a cylinder, three primaries reach a triangle, a colour is a name, a set and an inductive object, and the spectrum behind the colour is still there.

The conclusion · the rung's claim, in full, and its proof

This page opens once every question on 36j where it keys in is ticked. The claim below is proved from the steps of this rung, so it waits for them.

What the rung set out to show

The survey of rung 36 asked what a colour is. Its answer was a blindness: an eye keeps three coefficients of a spectrum and throws the rest away, and the three it keeps, mixed, make colours that no part of the band has. A colour is three numbers an eye keeps out of endlessly many, bent onto a circle so that the ends meet, and the spectrum behind it is still there.

36a made a spectrum a function, and 36b laid down the three curves and made a colour three coefficients. 36c found metamers and the purples, 36d bent the loop onto the circle of hue, and 36e filled it into the cylinder. 36f set three primaries inside the eye’s region, 36g made a colour a name, a set and an inductive object, 36h gave colours functions of their own, 36i kept the pair apart from the mix, and 36j keyed the rung into 30 to 35 and proved that spectra remain true. This page puts the six principles of the survey together as one claim.

The claim, in full

A spectrum is a function on the window, and its colour is its three coefficients against three fixed curves, so every colour is the colour of endlessly many spectra; mixing lights adds colours, the mixes of the band’s two ends are the purples that no wavelength has, and they close the spectrum into a circle on which a fully saturated colour is an angle; with brightness as a height and saturation as a radius the colours fill a cylinder whose axis has no hue and whose ends are black and white; three primaries reach a triangle inside the eye’s region and miss the rest in a known direction; a colour is a name, a set of spectra named by its triple, and an inductive object whose functions are 9a’s functions and at the corners 1e’s tables; and the spectrum behind a colour is still there.
