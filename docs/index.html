Mathematics from scratch

The ladder

A ladder is a tool that lets you earn height by taking steps. The Ladder is a tool that lets you earn techniques by taking steps, designed for high school and undergraduate academics.

If you're here then you've finished with intuitive math. Now you need to step into logic to earn the math that your intuition has led you through. The rungs start with a short break from arithmetic to introduce logic from the ground up, but before long you'll shoot past any peer who doesn't use the ladder. There's a lot of optional content to round out your climb. You'll learn the techniques required for success, and your learning will promote greater achievement through more ambitious goals.

You can stop when your STEM education stops, but the ladder goes to heaven.

You do need to clear each step’s questions, all but the challenges, before a rung’s conclusion opens: that is where the rung is summed up in the marks. Clear the challenges too, and the step wears a star.
