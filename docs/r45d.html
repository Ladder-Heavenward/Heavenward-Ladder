45d · proof

Handshakes

45c showed that a lossy link gives no certainty. This step builds what it does give: every message delivered, in order, once, with a chance as near 1 as you please. It is how the internet’s connections work.

Number every message, resend until it is acknowledged, and throw away repeats. Open with three messages, and read the numbers on a clock.

45d1Resend Until Acknowledged

The sender numbers each message. The receiver acknowledges each number it gets, keeps them in order, and drops any number it already has. The sender resends a message until its acknowledgment comes back.

Resend technique: if each try gets through and is acknowledged with chance at least p > 0, independently, then every message is acknowledged with chance 1.

The chance that k tries all fail is at most (1 − p)ᵏ, which falls below any positive number as k grows (41i). So the chance of never succeeding is 0.

45d2Three Messages to Open

To open a connection, each side picks a starting number. A sends its number a. B answers with its own number b and acknowledges a + 1. A acknowledges b + 1. After three messages, each side has the other’s starting number, and each knows the other has its own.

The starting numbers are picked fresh, not from 0, so that a stray message from an old connection is not taken for a new one.

45d3Numbers on a Clock

Sequence numbers have a fixed number of bits, 32 on the internet, so they count on a clock of 2³² hours (43a). After 2³² − 1 comes 0. To say which of two numbers is newer, the clock reads a as newer than b when the remainder of a − b by 2³² is less than 2³¹.

That reading is right only while fewer than half the clock’s numbers are in flight at once. So a fast link must not have more than 2³¹ unacknowledged numbers out.

45d4Closing, and the Generals Again

To close, each side says it is done and the other acknowledges. The last acknowledgment can be lost, as 45c promised. So the side that sent it waits a while before forgetting the connection, to answer a resent goodbye. Time and chance stand in for the certainty 45c ruled out.

The accepted names

This is TCP, the transmission control protocol. The opening is the three-way handshake, with SYN and ACK messages. Messages allowed out at once make the sliding window, and the closing side’s wait is TIME-WAIT.
