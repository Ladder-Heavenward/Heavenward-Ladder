38j · proof

Consistency from within

The argument of the last step had one premise that was not about codes: that the axioms of arithmetic do not clash. Outside arithmetic the ladder knows that, because the numbers are a world of the axioms. But the premise can itself be written as a statement of arithmetic, through codes: no number is the code of a proof of the totally false. Can the axioms reach that statement about themselves? If they could, they could run the whole of the last step’s argument inside, and reach the unreached statement, which they cannot. So they cannot reach their own freedom from clash, unless they clash, in which case they reach everything. A collection strong enough to describe its own proofs can never certify itself. This step proves it, from three facts arithmetic can prove about its own proofs, and asks what it does to the hope, old on this ladder, of proving once for all that mathematics does not clash.

Write “PA is clash-free” as a statement of arithmetic. If PA is clash-free, PA does not reach that statement, because from it PA would reach the unreached statement.

38j1Clash-freedom as a statement

From here on, Con is the statement ¬Prov(⌜⊥⌝): “no number is the code of a proof of ⊥ from PA”. It is true in N, since PA is clash-free.

38i1 proved, outside PA: if PA is clash-free, then PA does not reach U. In marks of arithmetic, that is Con ⇒ ¬Prov(⌜U⌝), and since U is reached as equivalent to ¬Prov(⌜U⌝), it is Con ⇒ U. The question is whether PA itself can reach that line. If it can, Con is out of reach: from Con, PA would reach U.

38j2Three things PA knows about its proofs

Three facts carry 38i1 inside. Write □φ as shorthand for Prov(⌜φ⌝), read “φ is reached”.

  • First: if PA ⊢ φ, then PA ⊢ □φ. That is the provable-mark technique (38h1).
  • Second: PA ⊢ □(φ ⇒ ψ) ⇒ (□φ ⇒ □ψ). Two proofs written one after the other, with one carry-through at the foot, make a proof. The code of the joined list is computed from the two codes by a recipe, and PA reaches the general fact by induction on the lengths, inside arithmetic.
  • Third: PA ⊢ □φ ⇒ □□φ. This is the first fact, proved inside PA: “if φ is reached, then the statement that φ is reached is reached”. Its proof is 38g4’s search-reached technique written out inside PA, by induction on the building of a bounded formula.

Held in trust. The second fact is part of the flag 38h1 left open until rung 40. The third is longer still: nothing new happens in it, and every line is a line of 38g4 carried inside, but the ladder does not write it out. The page uses all three.

38j3No self-certificate

No-self-certificate technique: if PA is clash-free, PA does not reach Con.

PA \vdash U \Rightarrow \neg\square U @@ the left-to-right half of U’s ⇔ (38i1)
PA \vdash \square U \Rightarrow \square\neg\square U @@ the first fact, on the line above, then the second
PA \vdash \square U \Rightarrow \square\square U @@ the third fact
PA \vdash \square U \Rightarrow \square\bot @@ 38j2’s last line, with φ = □U, and the two lines above
PA \vdash \neg\square\bot \Rightarrow \neg\square U @@ turn-and-deny (3a)
PA \vdash \text{Con} \Rightarrow U @@ Con is ¬□⊥; the right-to-left half of U’s ⇔
PA \vdash \text{Con} \Rightarrow PA \vdash U @@ carry an if through
\htmlClass{new}{PA \nvdash \text{Con}} \quad \blacksquare @@ PA ⊬ U, if PA is clash-free (38i1); turn and deny

The same lines run for every collection T of 38i3’s kind: if T is clash-free, T does not reach Con_T. A collection that reached its own Con would clash, and then it would reach Con too, along with everything else. So a proof of freedom from clash, from within, is worth nothing: only a collection that clashes has one.

38j4Where trust has to come from

6d held in trust whether the principles of sets are free of anything totally false, and 3c.4.4 asked what must be used or trusted to show it. Here is why that trust cannot be met from within. The axioms for sets of 38e4 hold PA, through ω and its adding, and membership in them is settled by a recipe. So if they do not clash, they do not reach their own Con. No proof from the ladder’s principles can show that the ladder’s principles do not clash.

PA \nvdash \text{Con} @@ 38j3, as PA is clash-free
PA \cup \{\neg\text{Con}\} \text{ is clash-free} @@ refute and not-not out, as in 38i4
M \models PA,\ \ M \models \neg\text{Con} @@ the full-growth and term-world techniques (38d)
\htmlClass{new}{M \text{ holds a member past every numeral that “codes a proof of } \bot \text{”}} \quad \blacksquare @@ as e did in 38i4; no number codes one

PA together with ¬Con does not clash, and it reaches a statement false in N. So “does not clash” is weaker than “true of the numbers”. Trust in PA comes from outside it: from N, built in sets on rung 4; or from a stronger principle, as 38·cb29 shows with a run past the endless. Each such proof moves the trust to the stronger system. It does not remove it.

The accepted names

The no-self-certificate technique is Gödel’s second incompleteness theorem. Con is Con(PA). □ is the provability operator of provability logic; the three facts are the Hilbert–Bernays–Löb derivability conditions, D1, D2 and D3. 38j.6.1 is Löb’s theorem. The hope 38j.6.4 asks about is Hilbert’s programme. A proof of Con(PA) by transfinite induction up to ε₀ is Gentzen’s consistency proof.

Who said it first

Kurt Gödel stated the second theorem in the paper of 1931 and gave only a sketch; David Hilbert and Paul Bernays wrote out a full proof in the second volume of Grundlagen der Mathematik (1939). Martin Löb gave the three conditions their tidy form in 1955. Gerhard Gentzen proved the consistency of arithmetic in 1936, by an induction along a well-order longer than any PA can reach, and Hilbert’s programme, set out in the 1920s, was reshaped around such proofs.

A proof that is not allowed

“Let T be PA together with Con. Then T reaches Con, so T reaches its own freedom from clash.” Not allowed: T’s own statement is Con_T, about proofs from T, and T reaches only Con, about proofs from PA. If T is clash-free, Con_T is out of its reach, and the trust has moved one step and not been met.
