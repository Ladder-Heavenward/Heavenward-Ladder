37·∎ · conclusion

A sound partly lost is partly told

A sound is a pressure that repeats. Its pitch is the repeat, its power the square, its quality the recipe. The ear drops the turns and what is hidden; a channel drops more. What arrives is a shadow of what was sent, told to within the noise, and a sound from a known family is told in full.

The conclusion · the rung's claim, in full, and its proof

This page opens once every question on r37j is ticked. The claim below is proved from the steps of this rung, so it waits for them.

What the rung set out to show

The survey of rung 37 asked what a sound is, and what an ear keeps of it. Its answer was a deafness: the ear keeps pitch, loudness and recipe and drops the turns and whatever a louder sound hides, and every channel between a mouth and an ear drops more. A sound partly lost is partly told: what arrives is a shadow of what was sent, exact to within the noise, and a sound from a known family is told in full.

37a made a sound a pressure obeying the wave equation, and 37b made pitch the rate of a repeat on a line of ratios. 37c made loudness the average of a square, counted in decibels, and laid down how the ear weighs and hides it. 37d made quality the recipe and found the ear deaf to the turns, 37e followed a sound as it fades and bounces, and 37f took a voice apart into a buzz and a tube. 37g made a sound a name, a set and an inductive object, 37h gave sounds functions of their own and asked which can be undone, 37i made rhythm a slow pitch and proved when two timekeepers lock, and 37j keyed the rung into 31 to 36 and proved the partly-told technique. This page puts the six principles of the survey together as one claim.

The claim, in full

A sound is a pressure p(x, t) that obeys p_tt = c²p_xx at one speed c = √(K/ρ) for its medium, with fλ = c; a sound that repeats every T is a sum of turnings at the whole multiples of 1/T and has pitch 1/T, placed on the line h = log₂(f/440) where intervals add; its intensity is ⟨p²⟩/(ρc), additive across frequencies, 20 log₁₀(p_rms/p_ref) in decibels, falling 6 dB per doubling of distance and 4.343α dB per metre; two notes are the same sound exactly when their recipes agree, and the turns change neither pitch nor power; at a boundary the share ((Z₂ − Z₁)/(Z₂ + Z₁))² bounces; a voice is a buzz times a tube whose hills lie at (2k − 1)c/(4L); a note is the smallest set holding one frequency and closed under octaves, and no pure fifth is in it; a filter can be undone exactly when its gain is nowhere 0, and a compressor with a finite ratio is undone by its expander; p against q meets once a bar when p and q share no divisor, and becomes the interval p/q when sped into pitch; two timekeepers lock exactly when |Δω| ≤ K; and from r = Ps + n with ‖n‖ ≤ ε the kept part Ps is told to within ε, the lost part not at all, and a member of a family whose kept parts lie more than 2ε apart in full.

So the rung’s claim holds: a sound partly lost is partly told, exact because a shadow is exact, and the part at right angles to the shadow is lost for good unless the family the sound came from carries the telling across. The laid-down lines, the laws of air, the window, the weighing, the bands, the deafness to turns, the vowel regions and the air’s share, enter as lines with their rates, and every conclusion about what an ear hears carries those rates to its end (37g5).

What the rest of the rung adds

Each step and annex after the proof steps adds a line to the claim. A line appears here once you have finished its page.

  • 37·phys: Air squeezed by a sound is stiffer than air squeezed slowly, so c grows as √T; a moving listener hears (c + v)/c times as often; a source faster than sound trails a cone with sin θ = c/v; and a bottle hums at (c/2π)√(A/(VL)).its line appears when you finish it.
  • 37·eng: A room rings for about 0.161 V/A seconds; along a chain of gear the decibels add between each stage’s floor and ceiling; and a loop from loudspeaker to microphone howls at a frequency where its gain reaches 1 in step.its line appears when you finish it.
  • 37·comp: Each bit of a sample buys about 6 dB; rounding after a random shake is right on average; an echo is a list fed back into itself; and a coder keeps only the bits the ear would not hide, about an eleventh of a CD’s.its line appears when you finish it.
  • 37·bio: The inner ear lays the pitch line along a strip, about 5 mm an octave, and hears more finely than its cells by the rate of firing as well; each 3 dB louder halves the safe time; a bat reads distance from an echo’s delay and speed from its shift.its line appears when you finish it.
  • 37·crypt: Flipping a voice’s band hides it only from ears; a voice taken apart into levels is locked by a random key read by remainders; a message in the last bit lies 90 dB down; and a voice played backwards keeps its colour and loses its words, which listeners told what to hear supply.its line appears when you finish it.

At the end of the rung you have only begun to hear how much a shadow can tell.
