The Machine and After

Computability, information, the machine made of switches, and the research fronts that are still open. On this page the form reaches the present day; the people without dates here are living, or their dates have not yet been revisited.

99 names, in order of birth. Each line says what the person did that the ladder uses; the links under it go to the steps where the work stands, with the name kept to the cellar there.
