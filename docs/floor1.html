Logic floor

Rungs 0 to 7. You start with plain English and the letters of the alphabet. Here every guess is one you are allowed to make: a few lines laid down and named, and then the long, patient work of seeing how far they reach. You leave able to say exactly what follows from what, and you will have argued with yourself often enough to trust it.

Here is the first surprise of the climb: for a while, the ladder puts numbers down. You know them from school, and they come back, built from nothing, on rung 3. Until then every line is made of names and statements alone, so that nothing is used before it has been earned.

Here is the second: you are already doing some of the techniques, you just don't know how they write them in math.

The rungs of this floor

  • 0 · thinking logically
  • 1 · making expressions
  • 2 · collecting and sorting
  • 3 · learning to keep going
  • 4 · operating over a series
  • 5 · making room to undo
  • 6 · learning to choose
  • 7 · fixing truth

At the top of the floor are its conclusion and its measuring stick. Passing it gives you the title logician, and the Analysis floor opens above.
