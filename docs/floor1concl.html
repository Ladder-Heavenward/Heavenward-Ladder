The floor conclusion · its principles, in the words and marks it built

This page opens once every step of the Logic floor is ticked. Its principles are proved on the rungs below, so it waits for them.

What the floor set out to show

The Logic floor opened with plain English and the letters of the alphabet. It promised that you would leave able to say exactly what follows from what, and that nothing would be used before it was earned. Numbers were put down at the start, and they came back on rung 3, built from nothing.

Here are the floor’s eight principles again, one to a rung, each in the words and the marks the floor built. Each links to the rung conclusion that proves it.

The principles, rung by rung

0 · thinking logically

Each thing is the same as itself, and sameness turns about and chains.

1 · making expressions

Each joining word has a tech that makes it and a tech that uses it, and those techs do all the reasoning.

2 · collecting and sorting

A set is nothing but its members.

3 · learning to keep going

An endless run can sit inside one set.

4 · operating over a series

Whatever is true at 0 and passes from each number to the next is true for every number.

5 · making room to undo

Every subtraction has exactly one answer, and so does every division except by 0.

6 · learning to choose

A picking with no rule exists only because the choice axiom is laid down.

7 · fixing truth

Every statement is so or not so, and choosing forces it.

What the floor shows

Count what the floor laid down. The self-sameness and swap axioms on rung 0. The axioms that say which sets there are, on rungs 2 and 3, with the infinity axiom among them. The choice axiom on rung 6. That is all. Every other line on the floor was earned, and each one can still be asked for its proof.

On the Logic floor, what you may write, and why, is never a matter of taste: a line is laid down by name, or it is earned by moves anyone can check.

Even the big ideas were earned. The counting numbers are one set, the smallest inductive one, and induction is a technique that follows from that. Rung 1 kept back the tech from ¬¬P to P. Rung 7 completed that foreclosure, from the choice axiom, and did not have to lay the either-way principle down.

So the floor demonstrates its own method. A short list of axioms, each named, each refusable. Above them, a growing kit of techniques, each a single move standing for the lines it took to earn. You have built the first floor of that kit, which is the smallest part of what it will hold.
