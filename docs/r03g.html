Raining, All the Way

Rung 3 learned to keep going. This step names the guess that keeps going with it: what holds at the start, and is kept by every step, holds however far the steps go, even without end.

If it holds at the start and every step keeps it, it holds all the way. A sunshower and a hurricane are both raining.

3g1Start, and Keep It

What holds at the start, and is kept by each step, holds of everything the steps reach. That is the shape of 3d’s inductive sets: a start, and a step that never leads out.

You do not follow the steps to check. You check the start once and the step once, and the guess covers the rest.

3g2A Sunshower and a Hurricane

A sunshower and a hurricane are both raining. One is a few drops in the sun, the other a storm that will not stop. The word does not care how many drops fall, or for how long.

Each drop is rain, and the next drop is rain too. That is all “raining” needs, so it holds of the light shower and of the storm alike.

Anexact, yet rigorous · the word that keeps going

A guess made of a start and a step holds however far the steps go. It counts none of them, and none of them can undo it.

3g3Even to Infinity

A process that never ends is no harder for the guess than one that stops. 3d’s axiom of infinity makes the whole endless collection one object, so the guess can be said of it all at once.

Nothing is looked at past the start and the step. The rest is carried.

3g4What It Costs

The step has to keep the claim every time, with no exception. One step that breaks it, and the guess stops there.

And the guess says nothing about an end, because there is none. It says what holds at every place the steps reach.
