endConclusion

The ladder trained your induction on paper: over arrangements, over series, and into plans. It left one kind to the world: induction into behavior, where a plan is carried out and what comes back is used.

You have been doing that kind all along. Guesswork is a kind of action: a guess is made, tried, and kept or dropped. Planning is a kind of action: a plan is something you do before you do the rest. All induction is a behavior: someone carries a pattern from the cases in hand to the next case, and the carrying is done, at some time, by you.

So you are already on the hook for induction into behavior. The steps of this rung write down in marks what you do when you act on what you have learned. The marks do not act for you. What you do next is the next case.
