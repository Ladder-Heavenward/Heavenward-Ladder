Endless floor

Past rung 45. There is no top, no measuring stick and no title. There is the next guess, and the one after it, for as long as you care to keep climbing.

The floor · read it through, then climb on

Introduction

Every floor below this one ended with a measuring stick. This one does not. Past rung 45 the ladder keeps going, but nobody has built the rungs yet, because nobody knows where they lead. What it carries are news flashes and research fronts: the news from every subject the annexes built, and the questions the field has not yet answered.

What you find here are the open questions: problems people are working on now, and some that have waited centuries. The research annexes on the floors below point here. The still-open annexes, from rung 33 on, are the doors.

The Endless floor is where the ladder stops teaching what is known and starts asking you to find what is not.

Everyone who climbs this far is a beginner again. That is not a failure of the climb. It is what the climb was for.
