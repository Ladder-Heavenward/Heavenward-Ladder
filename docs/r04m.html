Processes Without Things

Rung 4 built numbers as a series and operators on it. This step names what that buys: a space for guesswork that keeps only the processes, and can stay ignorant of the things that go through them.

The operator works on the process, not on the things. The things come back as measurements.

4m1The Operator Forgets the Things

2 + 3 = 5 holds of apples, stones and days alike. Adding works on the count, and never asks what is counted. The things have been taken out, and only the process is left.

That is why one proof about adding covers every pile of anything.

Anexact, yet rigorous · the process that forgets

An operator that ignores the things holds of every kind of thing. It is ignorant of what it counts, and right about all of it.

4m2Processes That Plug Together

The processes fit into one another. 4d defines by repeating, 4f builds adding from it, and 4g builds multiplying from adding. Each part was proved once, by induction over the whole series, and each plugs into the next.

So a guess about multiplying stands on guesses about adding, and none of them looks at a single thing.

4m3Bringing the Things Back

The things can come back, as measurements. 4k’s counting matches a pile of things with a number. The number then goes through the processes, and comes out as a count of things again.

The guess was made without the things. The measurement puts them back in.
