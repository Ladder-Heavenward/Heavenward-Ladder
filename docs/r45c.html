45c · proof

The Two Generals

Two generals must attack together or not at all, and their only link is a messenger who may be caught. This step proves that no plan, however long, is safe.

The sender of the last message cannot know it arrived, so it was not needed. Remove it, and the next, down to none.

45c1The Puzzle

General A alone knows whether to attack today. Every plan of messages must meet three demands. If A’s answer is no, nobody attacks. If it is yes and no messenger is caught, both attack. And never does one attack alone.

45c2Remove the Last Message

Last-message technique: in a plan that meets the demands, if a run ends with both attacking, then the same run with its last delivered message lost also ends with both attacking.

The sender’s view is the same whether the last message arrived or not, because nothing came back after it. So the sender acts the same, and attacks (45b2). The two never act apart, so the receiver attacks too.

Question

  1. 45c2.1understandWhat does the last-message technique say?
    Why does the sender act the same?

45c3Down to No Messages

Start from the run where A says yes and nothing is lost: both attack. Remove the last delivered message, then the one before, and so on. Each time both still attack, by the last-message technique. After finitely many steps, A says yes, no message arrives, and both attack.

\text{A says yes, nothing arrives: B attacks} @@ the end of the removals
\text{A says no, nothing arrives: B’s view is the same} @@ B has received nothing in either run
\text{so B attacks when A said no} @@ the same-view technique (45b2)
\htmlClass{new}{\text{the first demand fails}} \quad \blacksquare @@ no plan meets all three

Question

  1. 45c3.1understandWhy do both still attack after every removal?
    Why must B attack when A said no?

45c4What Is Left

Certainty is gone, but chance is not. Send k messengers, each caught with chance q, and all are caught with chance qᵏ (41i). Agreement then holds with chance 1 − qᵏ, as close to 1 as you like, and never equal to it. Every protocol on a lossy link lives here.

Question

  1. 45c4.1understandWhat does sending k messengers buy?
    Why is it never certainty?
