45h · proof

Agreement Despite Liars

45g’s machines may stop, but never lie. Here some machines may say anything: different things to different machines. This step proves how many honest machines it takes to outvote them.

With one liar among three, no honest machine can tell who lied. With more than three times as many machines as liars, quorums of two thirds always share an honest one.

45h1Generals Who Lie

A commander sends an order, attack or retreat, to the others. The demands: all honest lieutenants do the same thing, and if the commander is honest they do what it ordered. A traitor may send different messages to different generals, and may lie about what it was told.

45h2Three Cannot Handle One

With three generals, one of them a traitor, and messages that can be forged, no plan meets the demands.

\text{run X: commander honest, says attack to both; L2 lies, “it said retreat”} @@ L1 must attack: the commander is honest
\text{run Y: commander lies, attack to L1, retreat to L2; L2 honest} @@ L1’s view: “attack”, and L2 says “retreat”
\text{the views of L1 in X and Y are the same} @@ so L1 attacks in Y (45b2)
\text{by the same argument L2 retreats in Y} @@ L2’s view matches a run where L1 is the liar
\htmlClass{new}{\text{in Y the honest lieutenants disagree}} \quad \blacksquare @@ no plan works

Third-bound technique: with messages that can be forged, agreement despite f liars needs more than 3f machines.

Group any 3f machines into three blocks of f, and let each block play one general of the three. A plan for the many would give a plan for the three, which does not exist.

45h3Quorums of Two Thirds

With n = 3f + 1 machines, take quorums of 2f + 1. Two quorums share at least 2(2f + 1) − (3f + 1) = f + 1 machines, so at least one honest machine is in both. That honest machine will not vouch for two different values, and practical methods for liars are built on it.

45h4Signatures Change the Count

If every message is signed, as 43i makes possible, a traitor cannot forge what another general said. Lieutenant 1 can show the commander’s signed “attack” beside the signed “retreat”, and the commander is caught. With signed messages, agreement is possible with any number of traitors, given enough rounds.
