Under pressure

This rung is our attempt to symbolize induction into action. For its backbone it uses DBT, a comprehensive life-plan.

DBT is designed to improve coping in high-pressure situations. So the whole nook is designed to be used even without any other part of the ladder finished, so long as you are willing to tough it out.

The steps are symbolized. The questions are in plain text.

The first time a mark is used, its line says what it means in one sentence. That holds for "and", for "for all" and "for some", and for the syntax that changes how a line is read. Tap a line to see its reading and its Key.
