Advanced floor

Rungs 39 to 45. You arrive having seen the near edge of the field, which is all anyone has seen. Here the roads run out into country still being mapped, and the guesses are the kind the people at the front are making now. You leave with the questions the field is still asking, and a few of your own.

The floor · read it through, then climb on

Introduction

This floor follows several roads at once, each to the edge of what is known. Rung 39 builds a machine out of switches: true and false become 1 and 0 on a wire, and from them come a clock, a memory and a machine that follows a list, with a count of how long it takes. Rung 40 asks what any such machine can decide, and finds questions that no machine can answer. Rung 41 shows that counting alone can say how things connect.

Rungs 42 to 45 take plus and times on their own: on a clock, as rules standing alone, and as the key to an old question about formulas for roots. Rung 45 steps back and asks what mathematics is about, and answers with arrows.

On this floor, the ladder hands you the tools the field uses to ask its own open questions.

Many of the annexes here point at work still under way. Some of it was settled only recently, and some of it is not settled at all. You are close to the front now, and the front is always a beginning.
