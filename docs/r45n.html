Quorums by Hand

45g and 45h turned safety into one question: do every two quorums meet, and in enough honest machines? This step is the hand method for any scheme.

Add the two smallest quorums and take away n: that is the least they share. Then ask how many machines may fail with a quorum still alive.

45n3A Grid

Nine machines in a 3 by 3 grid. A quorum is one full row and one full column, 5 machines. Any two quorums meet, because the row of one crosses the column of the other. Quorums of 5 out of 9 are smaller, in a large grid, than a majority would be.

  1. 45n3.1understandWhy do two grid quorums always meet?
