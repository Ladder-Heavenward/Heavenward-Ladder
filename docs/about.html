About the author

The Ladder is written by Andrew Kowalski.

The author · and how to reach the author

Andrew is a career linguist who turned to mathematics as the universal language. The Ladder is a curriculum for mathematics from the eighth grade up, built one idea on the last, so that the craft of mathematics comes a little earlier in a life.

Inquiry has already been done into every kind of thing. The Ladder is a way to climb to it.

Reports

If you find a mistake, a confusing line, or a question that seems wrong, tap its address: the number at the end of a paragraph, the label of a question, or the seed.link address under a laundry question.
