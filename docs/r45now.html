45·now · research front

Who Runs Agreement Today?

The methods of 45f and 45g are not museum pieces. This annex says where they run now, and how their designs are checked before anyone builds them.

A small group of machines agrees, by majority, on who is in charge, and everything else trusts them.

45now1A Small Group That Agrees

Large systems do not run agreement among thousands of machines. They keep a small group, usually three or five, that agrees by majority (45f2) on a short record: who is the leader, which machine holds what. The rest read that record.

45now2Where It Runs

Raft runs inside etcd, the record that the Kubernetes container system keeps. Zab runs inside ZooKeeper. A Paxos group runs Google’s Chubby lock service. Each is a few machines that make many look like one.

45now3Checking a Design by Machine

A design for agreement can be written exactly and its runs searched by machine for one that breaks a demand, before any code exists. Search finds the rare bad interleavings that tests miss, which is 45a’s trouble with timing turned into a tool.
