44·∎ · conclusion

Arrows matter more than things

A category is things and arrows that run in a row. Maps of categories carry matched things to matched things, and natural maps are the ones made without choosing. A universal property fixes a thing up to exactly one matching, and a thing is known by the arrows into it.

The conclusion · the rung's claim, in full, and its proof

This page opens once every question on r44h is ticked. The claim below is proved from the steps of this rung, so it waits for them.

What the rung set out to show

The survey of rung 44 asked what is left of a thing when only its arrows are looked at. A thing of a category is pinned down, up to exactly one matching, by the arrows into it; so every construction given by a universal property is unique in the one sense that matters, and every statement made with arrows is carried across a matching. The price is that the build of a thing, which set it is, is not a question the arrows answer.

44a wrote down categories and matched things. 44b mapped categories and found that matched things go to matched things. 44c mapped the maps, and told the natural from the chosen. 44d pinned down products and sums, and 44e every limit and colimit. 44f tied freeing to forgetting. 44g proved a thing is known by its arrows, and 44h pinned the tensor product down by them. This page puts the claim together.

The claim, in full

In every category each thing has one do-nothing arrow, each undoable arrow one undoing, and being matched is a sameness relation; every map of categories carries matched things to matched things. A family of arrows closing every square is a natural map, natural maps run in a row and undo, and a space of finite dimension is matched naturally with its readings of readings, while over a field with more than three members no family matching it with its readings is carried along by every matching.

Any two things with the same universal property, a product, a sum, a limit or a colimit of one picture, are matched by exactly one arrow that agrees with the property; in Set the sift is a limit, classes are a colimit, and gluing is a colimit in Top. Freeing is left of forgetting, a left map carries sums to sums, and the natural maps from the arrows into A to any F match with F(A); so things with naturally matched arrows into them are matched. The tensor product of spaces of dimension m and n has dimension mn.

So the rung’s claim holds: arrows pin things down as far as anything can, which is exactly one matching, and a thing is known by the arrows into it. Held in trust: the gluing rule of 34i, which 44e read as a colimit, and on the steps after this page, that typed programs all stop, and that a program written for every type is carried along by every program.

That is how far arrows see. They see everything a thing does, and match any two things that do the same; they do not see which set a thing is, and the rung has argued that nothing a mathematician needs is lost. The advanced floor ends here, with the floor’s conclusion next.
